Why a multisig, and why this console.

One seed phrase is one point of failure. A multisig takes it away: several owners, and nothing moves until enough of them approve. Picoquorum is a new kind of owner to put in one, a small console with its own screen and a key sealed in a chip.

The videos are on their way. Nine short explainers are written and none is recorded yet. Until one is up, its script is here to read.

How these are made

Each script is a file in the repository, next to the docs it draws on, with a list of the claims to check again before recording. If a video says something the docs don't, the docs win.

Experimental. Early design. Use a testnet first and small amounts on mainnet. The Safe's threshold is the real control.
