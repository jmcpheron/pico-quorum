PicoQuorum · a concept for wallet hygiene

Wallet Facts

Every wallet is a recipe: some keys, some copies of them, and a list of things you trust. A label puts what matters most in big type at the top, and the trace amounts at the bottom.

These are mockups for discussion. The exposure levels are rough judgments, not measurements, and they describe kinds of setup, not any one company's product.

Read the sugars first

Three kinds of key

The label's sugar line says where each key came from and how many copies of it exist. That one fact decides most of what can go wrong.

Natural

Born in a sealed chip

The private key is made inside a secure element and never leaves it. There's no seed phrase and no copy anywhere, so there's nothing to leak. There's also nothing to restore from.

A PicoQuorum key, a wedgie.

Raw seed

Written down as words

Twelve or twenty-four words are the key. Every copy, on paper, in a photo or in a password manager, is the whole key. Backing it up and exposing it are the same act.

A browser wallet, a phone wallet, most hardware wallets.

Artificial sweetener

Synced by a platform

A passkey tastes like no seed phrase. But Apple or Google keeps copies on every device on your account and in its cloud. The backup is real, and it is your account.

Face ID or Touch ID passkeys, synced.

How to read a label

Big numbers first, trace amounts last

Serving sizeThe kind of money the setup is made for. The same wallet is fine for lunch money and wrong for savings.
Keys to move moneyThe calories. One key means one mistake can be the whole balance.
Exposure, then % at riskHow easy an attack is, and how much of the balance one incident of it can take.
Ingredients and ContainsEverything you trust, most important first, and the warnings people need to see.

Five setups, side by side

The first three are really one thing: a single secret, in a different wrapper. Look at the right-hand column. For one key, every attack that works takes 100%.

For Instant Wallet

The front of the box changes as you climb

Instant Wallet keeps one address while you add keys. A small front-of-box panel could show three numbers at each rung, with the full label one tap away.

Rungs from Instant Wallet's plan as of October 2026: a Face ID passkey first, recovery by the BuidlGuidl DAO after 7 days, your own recovery later, then a wedgie. The phone's default daily limit is 100 USDC plus 0.04 ETH. Those details are still moving.
